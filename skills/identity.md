This session is about the owner as a developer, not about any one product.

Read `identity/README.md`, `identity/technologies.json`, `identity/lessons.json`, and `identity/patterns.md`. Pull from `companies/*/company.json` → `developer` as needed.

Possible focuses: filling in the technology map, finding recurring themes across projects, where projects tend to stall and why, what they're strongest at, what they're been circling but hasn't committed to, or putting their work into words they're comfortable saying out loud.

Their lessons and reasons come from them. Ask, don't write them for them. You can point out patterns you see in the code and history, marked as `agent`.

Write back to the identity files with provenance, by `skills/writing.md`: their lessons quoted, your patterns marked `agent`.
